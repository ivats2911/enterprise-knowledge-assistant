mployee Onboarding Overview

Welcome to the organization. This document outlines the onboarding process for all new employees.

1. Company Introduction
Our company focuses on building scalable, customer-centric software solutions. We value ownership, operational excellence, and continuous learning.

2. Working Hours and Expectations
Employees are expected to work a standard 40-hour week. Flexible working hours are supported, provided business needs are met and communication remains clear.

3. Security and Compliance
All employees must adhere to company security policies. This includes protecting sensitive data, using approved devices, and following access control guidelines.

4. Tools and Access
New employees will be granted access to internal tools such as email, project management systems, and source control repositories during their first week.

5. Performance and Growth
Performance is reviewed quarterly. Employees are encouraged to set clear goals, seek feedback, and participate in learning and development programs.

6. Code of Conduct
All employees are expected to maintain professionalism, respect colleagues, and comply with ethical standards at all times.
